LIFE ABOARD WAYFARER

The little things.

Welcome home, technician.

Follow the project card to wake Pip, repair his wing, and meet Moss, Zip, and Alto. Then recover a cassette and teach the flock its tune.

Select a bird in the habitat or the flock bar. Give parts, whistle, open their LUMINA lattice, or chat. Watch them gather, recharge, build nests, and learn from each other.

Open Language & flock life to teach one bird or everyone, arrange visits, read their wordplay, and inspect partnerships, colonies, and family lines. Six conversations and twelve learned words qualify a bird to graduate. Friendly graduates can found colonies.

A graduate in a colony can build a hatchling with a second perch and three parts. The parent remains in the flock. Hatchlings inherit language and varied personality traits, then learn for themselves. There are eight berths, and parents rest twenty station minutes between nursery projects. Ready birds may also build a hatchling on their own.

Use World map to open one of six illustrated spaces. Portal buttons open neighboring rooms. Follow keeps the camera with your selected bird; Invite here asks them to travel to your current room as time advances. Pin worlds to watch their residents side by side below the habitat. Birds retain their words, families, and LUMINA structures across rooms.

The parts tray can build toys or share parts with friends. Click labeled objects in each world, or use the matching buttons below the habitat. Objects have costs and short cooldowns, and birds use them autonomously too. Watch their new thoughts and conversations in bubbles and the room transcript. Chat replies are read aloud by default, including while paused. Uncheck Read replies aloud below chat to mute them. Enable Voices for ambient bird conversations; private thoughts stay silent. The separate Sound toggle controls chirps.

Build turns bird parts or station supplies into permanent structures and upgrades. Birds with completed nests also build autonomously. New world creates a named, themed world with a portal connection for 8 station parts. You start with 24; salvage more in Build or donate bird parts. Click any object label to use it yourself or ask a resident bird. Structures, supplies, and new worlds are saved.

Your choices are saved automatically. The world rests when this page is closed or inactive; nobody suffers while you are away. Pause whenever you like.

Chat uses your local Qwen3 4B model through Ollama when available. Offline replies keep the story playable. Generated conversation may be imperfect; only game actions change repairs and inventory.

Prototype: six connected spaces and one cassette, with LUMINA growth and LonkWorld XC society. Their growth is a classical software simulation. Steam packaging and a larger campaign remain future work.
